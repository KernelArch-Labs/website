EXAKT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

EXAKT does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. We do not have a server that stores anything about you, because we do not have a server.

What stays on your device

Your progress — which levels you have cleared and your best move count on each — and your settings for sound and vibration are stored in your device's own local preferences. Nothing else is stored.

Your own cloud account, not ours

So that you do not lose your progress when you change phones, that same small amount of data can be copied into your own Apple or Google account:

  • On iPhone and iPad, through iCloud key-value storage, if you have iCloud enabled. Apple is the data processor.
  • On Android, through Android Auto Backup, which is part of the operating system. Google is the data processor.

We cannot see this data and we cannot retrieve it. It lives under your account, subject to Apple's or Google's privacy policy, and you can remove it from your account's storage settings. If you turn these features off, the game keeps working and the data simply stays on the device.

Purchases

EXAKT is free to play through level 40. Unlocking every level is a single one-time purchase. That purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play — we never see your payment details, and the only thing the app records is a single flag meaning "this player has unlocked the levels". Refunds and revocations are handled by the store.

The one network request

The app makes exactly one network request, once when it starts: it downloads a small static file that says which app versions are still supported, so that a seriously broken release can be stopped from being played. The request contains no identifier of any kind — no account, no device ID, no advertising ID, no query string, and no information about you. As with any web request, the server that hosts the file sees the IP address it came from. We keep no logs tied to you and use nothing from it.

If the request fails, the game plays normally.

Children

EXAKT is an arithmetic puzzle and is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data at all, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children. There is no advertising, no social feature, no chat, no user-generated content, and no external link out of gameplay.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and comparable laws you have rights of access, correction, deletion and portability over personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete. To remove the data on your own device, delete the app; to remove the cloud copy, use your Apple or Google account's storage settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. Any change that introduced data collection would also require updating the privacy declarations in both app stores, which are visible on the app's store listing before you install.

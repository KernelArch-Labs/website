EXAKT

You have a number and a target. Swipe, and your number slides across a board of +1, ×2, −1 and ÷2 tiles, taking each one it runs into, and the way you swipe decides which tile comes in next. Land on the target exactly: not close, exactly.

The daily puzzle

A new puzzle every day at midnight where you are, and the same for everyone. No hints or cards, every try is free, and your first clear is the one that counts.

A result a friend sends you looks like this:

EXAKT daily #17 · 9 moves · ★★★ · better than 77% https://www.kernelarch.com/exakt

That is puzzle number 17, solved in 9 moves for three stars, in fewer moves than 77% of the players who had solved it by then. It never shows the swipes, so the puzzle is still yours to solve.

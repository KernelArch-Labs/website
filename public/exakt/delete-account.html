EXAKT — Delete your account

Last updated: 15 October 2026

You can delete your EXAKT account and everything our server holds about you at any time. It is permanent and takes effect at once. The one thing kept for good is a one-way mark on each purchase, linked to nobody, so that no payment can be credited twice. Our logs are deleted as they age.

In the app — the quickest way

Open EXAKT, then Settings → Account → Delete account, and confirm. That deletes your account and its record on our server straight away. You do not need to write to us.

Without the app

Email service@kernelarch.com with the subject "Delete my EXAKT account", and either:

  • your Player ID, which the app shows under Settings → Account, with a button that copies it; or
  • if you signed in with Google, write from the address you signed in with.

We delete the account and its server record within 30 days and reply when it is done. You do not need the app, or to install it again.

If you only ever played without signing in and do not have your Player ID, your account holds no name or email, so nothing links it to you, not even for us. Its record on our server is erased by itself once nobody has used the account for 24 months, unless coins or hours of unlimited lives were bought on it and it still holds some: then it is kept, so that nothing bought is lost.

What is deleted

  • Your account: its identifier, and the email address and name your sign-in provider gave us.
  • Your record on our server: coins, lives, when unlimited lives end, the ad rewards of the last 8 days, your device's App Attest key, the fingerprints of purchases we credited, and everything else it holds.

One thing stays for good: a one-way fingerprint of each purchase you made, marked as already redeemed and linked to nobody, so that the same payment can never be credited again. For an hour, our server also keeps when the deletion happened, so that a sign-in from before it can't be used; then that is deleted too. Our logs, kept for India's CERT-In, keep what they hold about the account until they are deleted at 180 days old, or a year old from 13 May 2027.

Coins and lives cannot be restored once the account is deleted. Apple and Google keep their own records of any purchase you made in their stores, and AdMob keeps what it collects under Google's own rules; the privacy policy explains both.

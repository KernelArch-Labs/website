The daily puzzle

  • One level a day, the same for every player. It is free: playing it costs no life and no coins, and trying again costs nothing.
  • It has no prize. Its only result is how many moves you took, and how that compares with the day's other players, counted with no names: nothing of value can be had from it.
  • Our server counts a clear only once it has replayed the moves the app sends.

Rooms

  • A room lets up to eight players play the same levels against one clock. Rooms are free: joining or playing one costs no coins, and nothing else.
  • A room has no prize. Its only result is each player's place: nothing of value can be had from one.
  • Our server scores every level cleared in a room by replaying the moves the app sends, and its score is the room's.
